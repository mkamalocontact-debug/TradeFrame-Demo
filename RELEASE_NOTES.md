# TradeFrame Demo 1.00

Initial public Demo release for MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5.

## Included

- MT5 Demo-account build
- MT4 Demo-account build
- Installation notes and distribution terms
- Embedded interface graphics, logo, and sounds

## Highlights

- Draggable Entry, Stop Loss, and Take Profit controls
- Automatic position sizing from configurable risk settings
- Market and pending-order execution
- Live risk, reward, volume, and R:R display
- Hotkeys, market snap, chart fill, and sound feedback

TradeFrame Demo is restricted to demo accounts. It does not generate trading
signals and does not trade automatically without user action.
